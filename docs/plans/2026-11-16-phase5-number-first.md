# Media Tracker App, Phase 5 item 14 (Number-first rating): Plan

**Goal:** Dan types numbers he already likes; the app only helps with titles whose numbers are **close**. The typed (or imported) number is the truth. A "Which did you like more?" pick nudges a title by at most ±0.2 so close titles are ordered as picked. Tiers, the tier editor, Re-tier review, Lock and the Compare tab leave the UI.

**Wireframes (approved 2026-11-16):** `docs/design/mockups/RateClose.wf.dc.html`, `AddRate.wf.dc.html`, `Sharpen.wf.dc.html`.

**Replaces:** Phase 3c rulings Q6–Q8, Q13–Q19, Q23 (the tier fit, the shown-score rule, placement, Fix my ratings, Re-tier, tier editor, Lock). Still in force: Q9 (the Worker re-fits the set on every write that can move a score, one `json_each` UPDATE per table), Q10's "every read path tolerates old rows", Q11 (save payload; `moves` and `done` are accepted and ignored), Q12 (determinism), Q22 (deleting an entry deletes its comparisons), the optimistic `rating_rev` guard, D1 limits (≤ 100 bound parameters, ≤ 50 queries), 10 ms CPU.

## Rulings

- **N1 — the typed number is stored.** New nullable column `typed REAL` on `entries` and `seasons` (migration 0009, add-column only; nothing dropped). `typed` is the number Dan typed or imported; `rating` stays the **shown** number that lists, sorting, Top 5, Stats, friends and Export read. `tier`, `prior_mean`, `strength`, `score_locked` stay in the schema. Every model write sets `tier = tierFor(typed)` on the list's tiers (Profile and Stats tier counts keep working), `prior_mean = typed`, `strength = shown in hundredths / 100` (ties in the list sort) and `score_locked = 0`.
- **N2 — lazy backfill** (replaces `ensureTiers` on `GET /api/me`). A rated row with `typed IS NULL` gets `typed = rating` when it had no counted pick under 3c (`strength = prior_mean`, or either is null) or was locked. A 3c-fitted row gets `typed = round2(scoreOf(prior_mean, its tier's range))`. That is the number it was typed as, except that 3c clamped priors to 3–97% of the tier, so a fitted 10 comes back as 9.97. Every set that had such a row is then re-fitted under N4 and written in the same batch. This happens once per user: 2 queries when there is nothing to do, else ≤ 2 + 10 + 18.
- **N3 — close.** Two rated titles of the same rating set (a list, or a season tab) are close when their typed numbers are within 0.3 of each other, compared in whole hundredths: `|round(100·a) − round(100·b)| ≤ 30`. A pick **counts** when it is not undone, both titles are in the set and rated, and they are close **now**. So retyping a number drops the picks against titles that are no longer close. Those picks are kept and count again if the numbers come back close. Old 3c comparisons count the same way.
- **N4 — the nudge.** All of this is integer arithmetic in hundredths (T = typed), and the result is deterministic for any input order.
  1. *Moving titles:* the titles with at least one counted pick. Every other title shows exactly its typed number, so imported scores stay unchanged until a pick involves them.
  2. *Clusters:* the moving titles are joined when they share a counted pick, or when their T are within 40 of each other. Two moving titles in different clusters are therefore more than 0.4 apart and can never cross or tie.
  3. *Order inside a cluster:* Each title gets a strength: one Bradley–Terry gradient step from its typed number (strength = T/100 + Σ (y − σ(T_i/100 − T_j/100)) over its counted picks, y = 1 win, 0 loss, ½ "About the same"). A full `fitTier` solve is O(k³) per Newton step on a big pick group and would not fit the 10 ms CPU budget; the strength only chooses between titles that no pick orders and tells a tie. Kahn's topological order then runs over the edges *winner → loser* (every counted decisive pick), *higher → lower* for every counted "About the same" between different numbers (so a tie never reorders, even when another pick lifts the lower title), and *higher → lower* (every pair more than 0.3 apart). Pick and tie edges may be broken to resolve a cycle; the more-than-0.3 edges never are. Among the titles that are free to go next, the one with the highest strength goes first, then the higher T, then the lower id. When the picks form a cycle, the cycle is broken at the strongest title that has no unplaced title more than 0.3 above it. So the more-than-0.3 order is never broken.
  4. *Gaps:* consecutive titles in that order get a gap of G hundredths. The gap is 0 when the two have equal T and equal strength (within 1e-9). That is how two equal numbers called "About the same" stay tied. Otherwise G = 4, so the first pick between two 9.6s gives 9.62 and 9.58, as in the wireframe. If no assignment fits, G = 1 is tried, then G = 0 (weak order). G = 0 always fits.
  5. *Assignment:* with D_k as the running sum of the gaps, the values are z_k = T_k + D_k. The values are fitted non-increasing by pool-adjacent-violators (least squares) and clipped to the bounds U'_k = min over j ≤ k of (T_j + 20 + D_j) and L'_k = max over j ≥ k of (T_j − 20 + D_j). It does not fit when some L'_k > U'_k. The result is rounded half up to whole hundredths, and shown_k = round(w_k) − D_k. So |shown − typed| ≤ 0.20, decisive picks are honoured whenever the picks are acyclic and the box allows it, and titles more than 0.3 apart keep their typed order.
- **N5 — what is stored as `rating`** (the two-decimal rule, made exact). Titles sort by shown value (in hundredths, descending) and group by equal value. A group shows its exact value when the value is a whole tenth, or when the group holds an unmoved title (shown = typed; an unmoved title's `rating` is its typed number itself, so 8.25 or 9.97 stay as they are). Otherwise the group shows one decimal, `round1`, when three things hold: that tenth's text differs from the tenth's text of the nearest groups above and below; it is within 0.2 of every member's typed number; and the group holds no unmoved title. Otherwise it shows two decimals. Rounding never reorders titles and never makes two different values look equal.
- **N6 — questions after a save.** After a rating is saved in Add, entry detail or the seasons editor, the client asks `GET /api/ratings/:set/close/:id` (sign-in plus 2 queries). If "Ask me to compare close ratings" is on and the list is not empty, the close-call sheet opens. The list holds at most 2 questions: the titles close to the saved one, nearest typed number first (then title, then id), never a pair that already has a pick (not undone), and never a Plan to watch title. "Which did you like more?" offers two cover cards, "About the same" and Skip, with "1 of 2". Each answer is saved at once (`POST /api/ratings/:set/save` with one pick), so closing the sheet loses nothing. After the last question, the sheet shows the titles whose numbers moved, then Done.
- **N7 — Sharpen my ratings** (More). `GET /api/ratings/close-counts` returns the number of unanswered close pairs per enabled set (sign-in plus 1 batch of ≤ 10). The home lists every list with a count ("6 close calls" / "0 — all clear"). Picking a list loads `GET /api/ratings/:set/close`, which returns its unanswered close pairs, nearest numbers first, at most 200. The session asks them in order, "Close call 3 of 6", with Done, Skip and About the same. Each answer saves at once. A pair whose numbers changed in between (a retype elsewhere) is skipped if it is no longer close.
- **N8 — planned titles.** A title with status Plan to watch has no rating field (Add and entry detail). Its number, if any, is kept and still shows on its row. It is never asked about and is not counted in Sharpen.
- **N9 — Settings.** One switch: "Ask me to compare close ratings" (default on). It is stored in the existing `compareAsk`: on = `Both`, off = `Off`. The old values `Only for new ratings` and `Only when I start it` read as on and off respectively. Sharpen ignores the switch. The tier links, the tier editor, Re-tier review, Lock and the old Ask-to-compare radio leave the UI. `/compare`, `/retier/:set` and `/settings/tiers` redirect to `/sharpen` / `/settings`. `PUT /api/ratings/tiers/:list` is deleted, and stored tier sets stay inert. The compare queue is no longer filled: the close-call sheet replaces "Place it". Its routes stay for old clients.
- **N10 — edits.** `PATCH`/`POST` rating edits: a `rating` that differs from the stored **typed** number is a retype, and the new number becomes `typed`. `tier` and `scoreLocked` in a body are accepted and ignored (old clients). Every create, retype, clear, delete, import undo and save re-fits the whole set under N4 (it is cheap: sorting and small clusters) and writes only the rows whose `rating`, `typed`, `tier`, `prior_mean`, `strength` or `score_locked` changed.
- **N11 — Export my data** keeps its shape and adds `typed` to entries and seasons. Comparisons are still exported.
